linear_scale 7-Step BIS Certification Roadmap

Steps 1 → 7
1

Identify Applicable Indian Standard (IS Code) & Scheme

Day 1 – 3

Determine the exact Indian Standard specification for your product (e.g. IS 1239 for steel pipes, IS 10500 for drinking water). Check whether the product is under Mandatory Quality Control Order (QCO) or voluntary scheme. Choose between Scheme-I (ISI Mark) for manufactured goods and Scheme-II (CRS) for electronics.

✓ Search IS Code in Directory ✓ Verify QCO Notification ✓ Identify Product Group
2

Set Up In-House Testing Laboratory & Quality Infrastructure

Day 4 – 15

Set up all mandatory in-house testing equipment in accordance with the BIS Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT). Ensure all measuring gauges and apparatus possess valid calibration certificates from NABL-accredited labs. Appoint a qualified Quality Control Engineer.

✓ Procure SIT Test Equipment ✓ NABL Calibration Certificates ✓ Appoint QC Personnel
3

Submit Online Application on e-BIS Manakonline Portal

Day 16 – 20

Register at www.manakonline.in. Fill Form-V and upload plant layout, manufacturing machinery list, raw material test certs, in-house lab checklist, and Udyam Registration (for MSME subsidy). Pay nominal application fee (₹1,000; only ₹200 for Micro MSMEs).

✓ Portal Registration ✓ Upload Machinery & SIT Docs ✓ Claim 80% MSME Discount
4

Factory Audit & Preliminary Inspection by BIS Officer

Day 21 – 35

A designated BIS Technical Officer conducts an on-site audit of your factory premises. The auditor evaluates production capacity, verifies raw material sourcing, inspects in-house laboratory testing capability, and evaluates the competence of testing personnel.

✓ Factory Physical Inspection ✓ Live In-House Test Demo ✓ Inspection Fee ₹7,000/day
5

Sample Drawing & Independent Laboratory Testing

Day 36 – 50

During the factory audit, counter-samples are drawn and sealed by the BIS officer. One sample is tested in factory presence; the second sealed sample is dispatched to a BIS-recognized / NABL-accredited test laboratory for independent comprehensive testing.

✓ Sealed Counter-Sample Dispatch ✓ Track Status on LIMS Portal ✓ 50% Lab Subsidy in BIS Labs
6

Scrutiny of Test Reports & Grant of License (CM/L)

Day 51 – 60

Upon receipt of passing test reports from the independent laboratory and clearance of the inspection report, the BIS competent authority grants the Certificate of Conformity with an official 7-to-8 digit CM/L (Certification Marks License) number.

✓ CM/L Number Allotment ✓ Download Digital License Certificate ✓ Legal Right to Affix ISI Mark
7

Market Surveillance, Continuous Quality & License Renewal

Ongoing / Annual

BIS conducts periodic surveillance audits and draws samples from factory stock and open market retail stores. The manufacturer maintains complete SIT production registers and pays the annual minimum marking fee to renew the license every 1 to 2 years.

✓ Market Surveillance Samples ✓ Maintain SIT Quality Records ✓ Online Annual License Renewal

help Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Which products have mandatory BIS certification in India?

Products notified under Government Quality Control Orders (QCOs) require mandatory BIS certification. This includes structural steel (IS 2062), cement, packaged drinking water (IS 14543), gold jewellery (IS 1417), children's toys, electronic IT goods (CRS), helmets, and automotive tires. Selling these without BIS certification is illegal.

Q2: What is the difference between ISI Mark (Scheme-I) and CRS Registration (Scheme-II)?

ISI Mark (Scheme-I) requires both a physical factory audit and independent sample testing, granting a CM/L number for industrial, chemical, and building materials. CRS (Scheme-II) is a paperless self-declaration scheme for electronic/IT goods (laptops, mobile phones, batteries) based solely on NABL lab test reports without initial factory audit, granting an R-number.

Q3: How can Micro and Small Enterprises claim the 80% fee subsidy?

Manufacturers must hold a valid Udyam Registration Certificate issued by the Ministry of MSME. When submitting Form-V on Manakonline, selecting the Micro/Small category automatically applies the 80% or 50% concession on application and annual license fees.

Q4: Can foreign manufacturers apply for a BIS license?

Yes, under the Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS). Foreign factories must appoint an Authorized Indian Representative (AIR), pay FMCS audit charges, and send samples for testing in Indian BIS-recognized laboratories.

Q5: How is a BIS license renewed after expiry?

BIS licenses are initially granted for 1 or 2 years and can be renewed for up to 5 years via Manakonline before the expiry date. Renewal requires submission of production performance records, surveillance test clearances, and payment of the annual minimum marking fee.
